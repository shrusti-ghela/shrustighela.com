---
title: "Fine"
date: 2025-06-22
---

I don’t hate small talk. \
I hate when small talk is a mask. \
When it pretends to care but doesn’t. \
I hate talk that feels like it’s holding its breath. \
The kind that asks “How are you?” \
and hopes I say “Fine,” \
so we can both move on, \
untouched and unchanged.

If you’re just being polite, \
don’t. \
I’d rather sit in silence with someone real \
than swim in shallows with someone pretending.

Don’t ask me how I am \
unless you’re ready to know. \
Because I won’t give you the version \
that fits into a smile and a nod.

I’ve spoken for hours \
about how water tastes different \
in every city I’ve lived. \
How Vellore water feels dusty, \
Ahmedabad tastes like the pipes it travels through. \
Mehsana? \
A little salty, like it’s keeping secrets from the earth, \
whispering them through every sip. \
And Seattle? \
Soft. Almost apologetic. \
Like rain that forgot it had somewhere to be.

And Ayush, \
he listened, really listened, \
while I explained how \
I drink ice-cold water \
in places where the taste feels wrong, \
so cold it numbs the tongue, \
and I don’t have to lie to my own mouth.

Let’s talk about dreams. \
The ones you remember, \
and the ones you don’t dare say aloud. \
Tell me what book broke something open in you. \
What song made you feel seen. \
What food tastes like love, \
or like home, \
or like something you wish you could forget.

Let’s talk about the places you’ve never been \
but miss anyway. \
How your brother’s eyes light up \
when he talks about space. \
How your mom still tears up \
when she hears her wedding song. \
The kind of details \
that don’t make it into group chats or status updates.

Anu once pulled me into a rabbit hole, \
why orange the fruit \
and orange the color \
share the same name. \
And how apple and red never bothered.

Why? \
Who decides these things? \
And why don’t we question them more? \
Isn’t that strange? Isn’t that delightful?

We were told as kids \
that nothing rhymes with orange. \
But then Eminem, \
wild genius that he is, \
said: \
“I put my orange, four-inch door hinge in storage \
and ate porridge with George.” \
Like rhyme was a game of contortion, \
and he refused to lose.

I’ll tell you how in India, \
my favorite oranges were still a little green, \
tangy, sun-kissed, and defiant. \
And how here, \
mandarins try their best, \
perfectly orange, \
but somehow less alive. \
And how I didn’t eat mandarins for a year \
because they just looked too orange. \
Still, sometimes, \
if I close my eyes, \
they taste like home trying.

I love trivial things, \
as long as they are curious things. \
Joyful things. \
Real things.

Talk to me about math, \
about why prime numbers feel lonely. \
Talk to me about war, \
and the heavy silences between explosions. \
About history, \
the kind they forgot to teach us.

About psychology, \
why we cling to patterns that hurt us. \
About philosophy, \
the kind that keeps you up \
at 2:17 a.m.

Ask me how I’m doing, \
but only if you actually want to know. \
Because I’ll tell you. \
I’ll tell you in metaphors. \
I’ll tell you in a little too much detail. \
I’ll say weird when I mean lost, \
and tired when I mean trying.

So don’t ask unless you’re ready. \
And don’t expect me to lie \
to make you comfortable.

I don’t want weather reports. \
I want weather feelings. \
Thunderstorm confessions. \
Sun-drenched silences. \
The kind of talk that makes time irrelevant.

I don’t do well with surface. \
I crave marrow. \
Meaning. \
Mischief. \
Magic.

Don’t be polite to me. \
Be curious. \
Be chaotic. \
Be soft, sincere, a little unfiltered. \
Be kind, yes, \
but let it be the kind that’s real, \
not the kind that wears a mask. \
We don’t have to agree. \
We just have to care. \
Really care.

Come as you are. \
I’ll meet you there, \
with questions, \
with stories, \
with a glass of weird-tasting water, \
just slightly too cold.
