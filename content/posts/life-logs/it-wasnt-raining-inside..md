---
title: "It Wasn't Raining Inside"
date: 2025-06-09
---

Some friendships start in chaos, \
like 11th-12th grade coaching, where sleep was a myth \
and stress was the third friend in the room.

Shruti, you and I stuck like Fevicol back then, \
laughing, venting, trying to make sense of derivatives and the universe. \
Then undergrad came, and with it, silence. \
Life got loud in other ways. \
A trip. A few texts. \
Eventually, we became birthday friends, \
the kind who smile at a name on their phone once a year, \
and mean it, even if it’s brief.

Dhruvi, we started on the sidelines — just classmates. \
But when I moved away from Ahmedabad, something shifted. \
Suddenly, we were going on drives through quiet nights \
with Sicko Mode blasting in the car,  \
coffee runs that ran a little long, \
spicy noodles and pink sauce pasta at cafés \
that forgot our names but remembered our orders. \
You felt like home without trying.

Then came America, \
me in rainy Seattle, Shruti in Atlanta, Dhruvi in Boston. \
Scattered again.

But Shruti, you called. \
You visited. \
We walked through Golden Gardens park in the freezing cold \
like no time had passed, \
and maybe it hadn’t, not really. \
That visit stitched something back together.

And then, somehow, you both found your way to Seattle too. \
And I? I graduated straight into the void. \
No job. No plan. Just vibes (and not the good kind).

But you two, absolute clowns that you are \
somehow became my safety net. \
I didn’t worry about rent. Or food. Or even pretending to be okay. 

You didn’t just open your homes. \
You opened your hearts. \
You let me sit in the storm \
without asking me to act like it wasn’t raining. \
You gave me space to fall apart, gently.

And in that space, something grew. \
A paper. An idea. \
HALoGEN — born from frustration, too much coffee, \
and emotional support disguised as sarcasm. \
And it worked. \
Best Paper at TrustNLP. Accepted at ACL.

Because you two were around \
to roast me, feed me, \
and occasionally remind me I’m not completely useless.

That paper carries more than citations. \
It carries you. \
Your kindness. Your belief. \
The way you held me up without ever making it feel like a favor.

Thank you for being the kind of friends \
who show up, stick around, \
and make fun of you while doing it.

You made the mess survivable, \
and honestly, kind of hilarious.

Gratefully (but still judging you), \
Shrusti
